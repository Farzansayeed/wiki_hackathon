    About WikiExplore

    WikiExplore is a live, Wikipedia-powered encyclopedia reader. Every article, trend list, and historical event is fetched in real time from the free knowledge base — no static database, just the open web.

    Designed with simplicity and clarity in mind, WikiExplore layers a calm editorial reading experience over Wikipedia's vast repository: live search, trending feeds, reading history, and a distraction-free article view.

    Live content

    Real Wikipedia articles, feeds and events — nothing stored, always current.

    Reader-first

    Editorial serif typography, font controls, progress tracking and print view.

    Personal library

    Bookmarks, reading streaks and one-click JSON exports of your history.
